About

An analyst who builds the system

I hold a master's degree in biotechnology from Khon Kaen University and moved into software through the Junior Software Developer programme at Generation Thailand.

I work as a business analyst and system analyst alongside development: eliciting requirements, modelling architecture and data, planning tests, and producing documentation where every requirement can be traced.

My current interest is analysing and designing AI-enabled systems, with user privacy treated as a design precondition rather than an afterthought.

Honest limitations

This project was carried out by one developer under simulated roles, so the figures do not come from field research with real users. Testing with real users has not started yet, and the image generation feature is limited by the AI provider's quota. Every limitation is recorded in the documents rather than glossed over.

Part two · System design

From requirements to architecture you can check

The other half answers what the system is made of, how the data stays safe, and why each choice was made. All of it sits in documents D-10 to D-20.

01

Isolation lives in the database

Four RLS policies bound to auth.uid(), so an application bug cannot return another user's row.

02

Vector-first search

1,536-dimension vectors on pgvector with an HNSW index and a keyword weight in one function.

03

A visible privacy boundary

Every path that sends text to the AI provider has a consent check in front of it.

04

Every decision recorded

Ten decision records naming the alternatives considered and why they lost.

Method

Four principles behind every document

01

Agree before writing

Every document starts by agreeing the scope and the definition of done, before drafting begins.

02

Trace every line

Problem, requirement, use case, screen, API and test case are linked in a single matrix.

03

Quality must be measured

Quality requirements are numbers, such as p95 within 3.5 seconds at 5,000 entries.

04

State limits plainly

Weak points and unfinished work are recorded with a plan, neither hidden nor overstated.
